About Me

Data. ML. Agents.

I'm Harry: engineer and scientist by training, business-minded by choice. 8+ years across Southeast Asia's most recognisable tech platforms: Shopee (ecommerce), Gojek (ride-hailing) and Vidio.com (OTT). At Shopee Indonesia I ran BI Engineering and owned the whole chain: data lake and warehouse foundations, dimensional models, standardised metrics, self-service datasets, governance, and the ML on top.

I grew that team from 4 to 14+ engineers and set its direction: self-service data mart adoption rose from ~5% to ~50%, governance coverage from 13% to 58%. I owned the secure, automated pipelines behind ShopeePay's mandatory Bank Indonesia and OJK regulatory reporting, and worked closely with Marketing & Ads on loyalty, gamification, Shopee Ads, Shopee Live & KOL and P&L, including the customer profiles behind their funnels. Great data leadership isn't about dashboards; it's the policies, standards and trusted metrics that let every team make autonomous decisions without waiting on an analyst.

The other half of the job is machine learning and AI. I've led end-to-end ML delivery across fraud detection, user profiling and computer vision, and saved ~$500K in manpower costs with LLM and agent automation I built hands-on. Now completing a Master of Data Science at USyd to keep that edge sharp and lead the move from reporting to decision intelligence: ML that decides and agents that act.

$ status → Open to Applied AI & Decision Systems roles.

Leadership

How I lead data & AI teams

Two beliefs run through everything I've built: data should be democratised, and AI is there to augment the people using it. I became a manager the slow way, by building the systems first, so I can still read the pull request and know which corners are safe to cut.

01
Data belongs to everyone

Every team should be able to answer its own questions without queuing behind an analyst. That is a delivery problem, not a slogan: data taken the whole way from raw to mart, at a quality people rely on, and easy enough that they choose it over their own spreadsheets.

Self-service adoption ~5% to ~50%
02
Democratised data has to be trustworthy

Opening access to wrong data is worse than keeping it shut. Before I widen the door I want documented assets, standardised metrics, approved access paths and ISO-aligned security. Governance is not bureaucracy: it lets me say yes to more people, faster, and it makes AI possible later.

Governance coverage 13% to 58%
03
AI is there to augment people

Owning the whole lifecycle, from raw ingestion to the mart, I know clean, trusted data is the engine AI runs on; that is where the two halves of my work meet. I put AI where it takes manual work off people, not where it replaces them, so teams spend their hours on what only humans can do.

~$500K of manual work automated
04
Stay close enough to build

I do not review architecture from a slide. I still write code and deploy things, which lets me size a project honestly, spot an estimate that is fiction, and keep the trust of engineers who know when a manager has drifted.

Shipping code in 2026, not just reviewing it
