About Me

BI end-to-end, from warehouse to AI

I'm Harry, a Business Intelligence & Data Leader with 8+ years across Southeast Asia's most recognisable tech platforms: Shopee (ecommerce), Gojek (ride-hailing), and Vidio.com (OTT). I ran BI Engineering inside Shopee Indonesia's Business Intelligence function, where I owned the whole chain: data lake and warehouse foundations, dimensional models and standardised metrics, self-service datasets, governance, and the ML that sits on top of it.

I grew that team from 4 to 15+ engineers, lifted self-service data mart adoption across the organisation from ~5% to ~60%, and raised governance coverage from 13% to 58%. I also built the secure, automated pipelines behind ShopeePay's mandatory Bank Indonesia and OJK regulatory reporting. Great BI leadership isn't about dashboards. It's establishing the policies, standards and trusted metrics that let every team make autonomous decisions without waiting on an analyst.

The other half of the job is AI. I've led end-to-end ML delivery spanning fraud detection, user profiling and computer vision, and saved ~$500K in manpower costs through LLM and agent-based automation. I'm now completing a Master of Data Science at the University of Sydney to keep that edge sharp, so I can lead a BI function that doesn't stop at reporting, but ships intelligence into production.

$ status → Open to research collaborations and exciting opportunities.
